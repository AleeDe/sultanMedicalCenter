# Demo Video Script — Client Presentation

**Length:** ~2 min 30 s · **Audience:** clinic owner deciding whether to buy
**Narration:** Roman Urdu · **Subtitles:** burned in, same text

Each scene lists the narration line, roughly how long it runs, and what the
screen must be doing while it is spoken. Timings are targets — the recorder
paces the UI to the audio, never the other way round, so nothing is ever
narrated before it is visible.

---

## 1 · Opening (0:00 – 0:12)

> **VO:** Yeh hai aap ke clinic ka Token aur Billing system. Reception se
> lekar bill tak, sab kuch ek jagah.

**Screen:** New Token screen, empty. Slow zoom-in.

---

## 2 · Emergency triage (0:12 – 0:28)

> **VO:** Sab se pehle — patient normal hai ya emergency. Ek click, aur poori
> screen ka rang badal jata hai. Door se bhi pata chal jata hai ke emergency
> token ban raha hai.

**Screen:** Click Emergency — whole screen repaints red, fee jumps to 1500.
Pause. Click back to Normal OPD.

*This is the strongest single moment in the product; give it room.*

---

## 3 · Returning patient (0:28 – 0:48)

> **VO:** Purana patient hai? Sirf phone number daalein. Naam, umar, sab kuch
> khud bhar jata hai — dobara likhne ki zaroorat nahi.

**Screen:** Type a phone number that exists, press Enter. Green "Known
patient" banner appears, fields fill themselves.

---

## 4 · Doctor selection (0:48 – 1:03)

> **VO:** Doctor chunein — naam, speciality aur room number. Yeh sab parchi
> par print hota hai, to patient ko pata hota hai kahan jana hai.

**Screen:** Click Dr. Sara Iqbal. Tick appears. Slip preview on the right
updates to show the doctor and room.

---

## 5 · Labs at the counter (1:03 – 1:25)

> **VO:** Agar doctor ne test likhe hain, wo abhi add kar dein. Blood test,
> X-ray, jo bhi chahiye. Total khud jama ho jata hai — patient ek dafa paisay
> deta hai, ek hi parchi milti hai.

**Screen:** Open the lab picker, tick CBC and X-Ray Chest. Chips appear.
Fee panel shows 500 + 2000 = **Total to collect Rs. 2500**.

---

## 6 · Issue and print (1:25 – 1:42)

> **VO:** Token banayein. Number itna bara ke door se parha jaye, aur parchi
> apne aap print ho jati hai.

**Screen:** Click Issue Token & Print. Big token number animates in. Then show
the printed slip artwork with every line — OPD fee, CBC, X-ray, total.

---

## 7 · Billing ledger (1:42 – 2:02)

> **VO:** Baad me koi test add karna ho, to Billing me jayein. Har item alag
> alag — abhi paisay lein ya baad me. Aakhir me ek bill, ek total.

**Screen:** Billing screen with a visit open. Add an item, show PAID vs
pending, then the running total on the right.

---

## 8 · Analytics (2:02 – 2:22)

> **VO:** Aur malik ke liye — poora hisaab. Kitni aamdani, kitne patients,
> kaun sa doctor kitna busy, kaun se test zyada chalte hain. Pichle mahine se
> muqabla bhi.

**Screen:** Admin → Analytics. Slow scroll: KPI cards, revenue chart, busiest
hours, by-doctor ranking.

---

## 9 · Close (2:22 – 2:35)

> **VO:** Tez, saaf, aur seekhne me asaan. Aap ke clinic ke liye tayyar.

**Screen:** Return to New Token screen, clean and empty. Fade out.

---

## Recording notes

- Every action gets a visible pause before and after; the cursor moves at
  human speed, not instantly.
- The mouse pointer is drawn in, since a demo without a visible cursor is hard
  to follow.
- Real data throughout — the seeded 45 days of history — so the analytics
  screen looks like a working clinic rather than an empty template.
